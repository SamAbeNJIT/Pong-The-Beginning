A tiny tower defense, but really polished. One winding path across a single-screen grid. Keyboard play: arrow keys move a build cursor on the grid, 1/2/3 place one of three towers (a fast Blaster, a splash Mortar, and a Frost tower that slows), U upgrades the tower under the cursor (two upgrade levels, visibly bigger and stronger each time), S sells it for a partial refund, and Space sends the next wave early for bonus gold. Mouse clicks may also place towers, but keyboard alone must fully work. 10 waves with a clear preview of what is coming next: fast runners, armored tanks, swarms of tiny creeps, healers, and a boss on wave 10. Earn gold per kill; each enemy that reaches the end costs a life (20 lives). Show range circles, health bars, damage numbers, satisfying hit and death effects, screen shake on the boss, synthesized sound for every action, and a clean HUD for gold, lives and wave. Win by surviving wave 10; lose at 0 lives.
